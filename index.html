A Journey of Passion, Logic & Growth

I was born on 14th July 2007 in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh—a city celebrated worldwide for its timeless Tehzeeb (grace), rich literary heritage, and architectural splendor. Growing up in this environment gave me a deep appreciation for detail, poise, and artistry.

At the same time, the logical world of computing fascinated me. During my foundational schooling at The Lucknow Public Collegiate, I was introduced to C++, HTML, and SQL. While those were initial stepping stones, they sparked a fire: the thrill of transforming abstract logic into working software.

Today, as a 1st-year Bachelor of Technology student in Computer Science & AI at Rishihood University, I am channeling that enthusiasm into structured learning. I have embarked on an intensive journey with Python, laying down strong foundations in algorithms, analytical thinking, and future-forward AI tools.
